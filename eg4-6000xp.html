EG4 6000XP Overview

The EG4 6000XP is a 48V split-phase off-grid inverter/charger designed for solar-plus-battery applications. EG4 lists 6kW nominal output and up to 8kW of usable PV input, with two MPPTs and 120/240V split-phase output.

The unit can operate off-grid and can also use grid or generator input for supplemental charging. EG4 also supports parallel operation, allowing up to 16 units according to its current product documentation.
