10kW Hybrid Solar System Price in India With Battery

A 10kW hybrid solar system combines solar generation with an inverter capable of working with battery storage. It is more expensive than a basic on-grid installation because the battery and battery-compatible inverter add substantial cost.

Current 2026 market guides commonly put a 10kW hybrid system with battery around ₹8.5 lakh to ₹11 lakh, with the final figure strongly dependent on battery chemistry and usable storage capacity. A larger lithium battery bank can push the project above this range.

The panel count depends on the selected wattage. For example, 18 × 550W panels provide 9.9kW, while 17 × 590W provide about 10.03kW. How Much Electricity Can a 10kW Solar System Generate in India?

Production depends on state, city, season, orientation, tilt, shading, temperature, dust and equipment losses. A practical planning range for a 10kW rooftop array is roughly 35–45 kWh per day on an annualized basis in a good solar location, with much higher or lower individual days possible.

Annual output can therefore be around 12,500–16,500 kWh per year as a broad planning range. This is not a guarantee; a professional installer should use local solar-resource data and site conditions to model expected generation.

A 10kW rooftop installation may require roughly 700–1,000 square feet of usable shade-free roof area, depending on module dimensions, row spacing and maintenance access.

PM Surya Ghar Subsidy and 10kW Systems

The Government of India's rooftop programme provides central financial assistance for eligible residential grid-connected rooftop solar. Current published guidance indicates that the residential CFA is capped at ₹78,000 for systems above 3kW under the PM Surya Ghar framework. Eligibility, approved equipment, application route and DISCOM verification matter.

Importantly, do not assume that every 10kW hybrid, off-grid or battery-based system automatically receives the same subsidy. The subsidy is tied to eligible residential grid-connected rooftop configurations and the applicable scheme rules. Commercial systems are treated differently.

Buying tip: If subsidy eligibility matters, ask the installer to show the exact scheme pathway, approved vendor status, module/DCR requirements and the amount expected to be credited after installation and verification.

Solar Plate Price vs Solar Panel Price in India

Indian buyers often search for both “solar plate price” and “solar panel price.” In everyday usage these terms usually refer to the same photovoltaic module. The useful comparison is the module's wattage, efficiency, technology, warranty, certification, DCR status where applicable and price per watt.

10kW Solar Inverter Price in India

A 10kW inverter can vary widely in price. On-grid string inverters are generally less expensive than hybrid inverters because hybrid models add battery-management and backup functions. Before choosing an inverter, check MPPT count, maximum PV voltage, maximum PV current, AC output, battery voltage compatibility, monitoring, warranty and whether the intended grid connection is single-phase or three-phase.

10kW Solar Battery Price in India

Battery cost is the biggest variable in a hybrid system. Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries are common in modern storage systems, but the correct battery should be selected based on usable kWh, continuous power and the inverter's approved battery ecosystem rather than simply the advertised kWh number.

What to Check Before Buying a 10kW Solar System in India

  1. Get the exact panel model and wattage in writing.
  2. Check whether the modules are DCR where required for the intended subsidy route.
  3. Compare price per watt, not just price per panel.
  4. Check the inverter's maximum PV voltage and current.
  5. Ask for the battery's usable kWh, not only nominal capacity.
  6. Confirm structure material, cable sizes, SPD, breakers and earthing.
  7. Check installation, transport and civil-work inclusions.
  8. Compare product warranties and local after-sales support.
  9. Confirm DISCOM/net-metering requirements for your location.
  10. Request a complete written quotation before paying an advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the solar panel price in India in 2026?

Indicative retail pricing varies roughly from the mid-teens to the mid-thirties of rupees per watt depending on technology, DCR status, brand and market channel. TOPCon and bifacial panels generally cost more than basic legacy modules.

What is the 10kW solar system price in India?

A broad 2026 planning range for an installed 10kW on-grid system is around ₹5.5–₹7.5 lakh before considering eligible subsidy. Location, equipment and installation can move the final price significantly.

What is the 10kW hybrid solar system price in India?

A common market-planning range is around ₹8.5–₹11 lakh with battery storage. Larger lithium battery capacity can take the total higher.

How many 550W panels are needed for 10kW?

18 × 550W panels give 9.9kW of DC capacity. The final design depends on inverter limits and string configuration.

Does a 10kW system get the full PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

Eligible residential grid-connected rooftop systems above 3kW are subject to the applicable CFA cap, currently published at ₹78,000. Do not assume every hybrid or off-grid system qualifies; verify the exact configuration and application route.

Is a 10kW hybrid solar system good for a large home?

It can be suitable for a large home with high daytime consumption and a need for backup, but battery size and peak load should be designed around the home's actual appliances and outage requirements.
