Solar Panel Prices by Country in September 2026: Price Per Watt & 400W–630W Rates

Solar panel prices are not the same everywhere. Brand, technology, local taxes, shipping, distribution and installation markets can change the final price. This SolarRateHub guide compares indicative panel-only price-per-watt ranges across 10 countries outside Pakistan and converts those ranges into easy 400W, 500W, 545W, 580W and 630W panel estimates.

Solar panel price per watt in September 2026

The most useful way to compare panels of different sizes is price per watt. A 630W panel may cost more per panel than a 400W panel, but it can still be cheaper on a per-watt basis. For that reason, buyers should compare both the $/W or local-currency/W rate and the total panel price.

400W solar panel price in 2026

A 400W panel remains a useful reference point because it makes the price-per-watt calculation easy. In the U.S. planning range above, a 400W panel at $0.27–$0.40/W works out to about $108–$160 before other project costs. In the UK range, the same calculation gives about £52–£80.

Actual 400W products may be older-generation residential modules in some markets, while newer homes often use 430W–500W or larger modules. Always check the exact model and physical dimensions.

500W solar panel price in September 2026

500W is a useful mid-size benchmark for comparing residential and larger rooftop modules. It also makes price-per-watt comparisons easier across countries. Based on the indicative ranges above, a 500W module works out to around $135–$200 in the U.S., £65–£100 in the UK, S$125–S$225 in Singapore and ¥325–¥525 in China.

545W solar panel price

545W modules are widely useful for modern high-power rooftop and commercial applications. The calculated September 2026 planning range is about $147–$218 in the U.S., £71–£109 in the UK, S$136–S$245 in Singapore and ¥354–¥572 in China. Availability depends on brand and local distributor stock.

580W solar panel rate

580W is a popular reference point for large-format N-type and bifacial modules. Higher wattage can reduce the number of panels needed for a target system size, but the installer should also check module dimensions, roof layout, inverter MPPT limits and string voltage.

630W solar panel price

630W modules are particularly useful when a project wants fewer modules for the same DC capacity. They are more common in large-format commercial, ground-mount and spacious rooftop applications than in every small residential market. The table's 630W prices are calculated estimates from each country's indicative price-per-watt range, not a statement that a specific 630W model is locally available.

Before buying a 630W or other large-format panel:
  • Confirm the panel's physical dimensions and weight.
  • Check the inverter's maximum DC voltage and current.
  • Confirm the mounting structure is compatible.
  • Check whether the installer can handle the panel size safely.
  • Compare warranty, degradation and product certification, not only watts.

Why solar panel prices differ by country

  • Local taxes and VAT: Retail prices may include or exclude different taxes.
  • Shipping and logistics: Freight, warehousing and inland delivery can materially change a panel's final price.
  • Import rules and tariffs: Some markets have different treatment for imported modules and local manufacturing.
  • Supplier margin: Dealer and installer margins vary by market and order size.
  • Technology: TOPCon, HJT, back-contact and glass-glass modules can have different price points.
  • Panel size: Large-format modules can be cheaper per watt but harder to handle on small roofs.
  • Exchange rates: A change in the local currency can move today's solar panel rate even when the factory price stays stable.

What current 2026 market data tells us

Recent U.S. wholesale data gives a useful benchmark: a Q3 2026 index based on more than 4,300 in-stock panel SKUs reported a median wholesale price of $0.27/W, with the 500–549W band at about $0.20/W. That is a wholesale benchmark, not a homeowner retail quote, so local retail pricing can be higher.

UK supplier observations in 2026 show mainstream 455–490W modules around £62–£74 ex VAT and 535–610W modules around £72–£79 ex VAT in one supplier sample. In Germany, Q3 2026 wholesale spot ranges for premium TOPCon were reported around €0.10–€0.14/W, while retail/installer pricing is higher.

The Netherlands is a good example of why the phrase “price per watt” needs context: one 2026 installer-market comparison puts several Tier-1 Chinese brands around €0.38–€0.42/W before VAT, which is an installer/module price reference rather than an international factory price.

Panel-only price vs complete solar system price

Do not multiply a panel price by the number of panels and assume you have the cost of a solar system. A complete system can also include the inverter, mounting structure, DC and AC cables, breakers, surge protection, earthing, monitoring, labour, scaffolding, permits and grid-related work. A battery adds another major cost category.

Final takeaway: solar panel rate today in September 2026

There is no single worldwide daily solar rate. The most useful benchmark is the price per watt for a comparable panel class, followed by the exact panel price, taxes, delivery and installation. For September 2026, the international ranges in this guide provide a practical starting point for comparing the United States, United Kingdom, Singapore, Hong Kong, China, Romania, Thailand, Germany, Netherlands and Brazil.

If you are checking the today solar rate before buying, ask the supplier for the exact model number, wattage, technology, warranty, included taxes and delivery terms. Then compare the quote using price per watt rather than looking only at the price of one panel.
