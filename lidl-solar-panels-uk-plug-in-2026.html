Should you wait for Lidl?

If your main goal is the lowest possible purchase price, waiting for a confirmed Lidl UK kit could make sense. Lidl's German range demonstrates its ability to sell compact plug-in systems at aggressive prices. But there is no reason to buy an uncertified imported kit just because it looks similar.

If you want solar now, compare a currently compliant UK product instead. The key check is the exact model—not just the brand name—and its status on the ENA register.

How much can plug-in solar save?

The government estimates savings of up to £110 per year for households using plug-in solar, while stressing that results vary. citeturn0search0

The biggest factor is self-consumption. Solar electricity generated while appliances are operating can offset grid electricity directly. If generation exceeds the home's demand at that moment, the financial value of the extra electricity can be lower. Therefore, a south-facing panel arrangement and daytime loads such as a fridge, router, computer or other appliances can improve the economics.

For a simple payback estimate, divide the kit price by realistic annual bill savings. A £400 kit saving £80 per year would have a simple payback of about five years; a £600 kit at the same saving would take about 7.5 years. These are illustrative calculations, not guaranteed returns.
