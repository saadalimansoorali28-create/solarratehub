2 plug-in solar panels Lidl: how much power?

A two-panel balcony kit can feed a microinverter that supplies electricity to the home's circuit. The panels' combined DC rating can be above the permitted AC output. Under the current UK socket-connected route, the output limit is 800W.

The UK government says plug-in solar kits can provide up to 800W and estimates potential savings of up to £110 per year, with generation potentially covering up to 20% of average home electricity use. Real savings depend on sunlight, orientation and how much electricity the household consumes while the panels are generating.

UK plug-in solar rules in 2026

Plug-in solar became legal across Great Britain from 27 August 2026, subject to the applicable product and network requirements.

  • Socket-connected output can be up to 800W.
  • The exact product must meet the applicable UK safety requirements.
  • Check the exact model on the ENA G98 Type Test Register.
  • Registration/notification requirements still apply.
  • Do not assume that a product being advertised online means it is approved for UK socket connection.

The government's electrical safety study found tested plug-in PV systems could operate safely under the tested conditions, while also identifying differences in product performance and the need for a UK-specific product specification.

Should you wait for Lidl?

If your main priority is a low purchase price, waiting for an official Lidl UK kit could be reasonable. Lidl's German range shows that the company already understands the balcony-solar format.

If you want plug-in solar now, use a currently compliant UK product instead. The important check is the exact model, not simply the brand name.

How much can plug-in solar save?

The government estimate is up to £110 a year, but actual savings vary. A household that uses electricity during sunny daytime hours can generally make better use of its solar generation than a home with very little daytime demand.

For a simple payback calculation, divide the kit price by realistic annual savings. For example, a £400 kit saving £80 per year has a simple payback of about five years. A £600 kit at the same saving would take about 7.5 years. These are illustrations, not guaranteed returns.
