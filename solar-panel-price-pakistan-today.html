Why Solar Panel Prices Are Different

Brand and Model

Two panels from the same brand can have different prices because their series, cell technology, wattage and construction differ.

N-Type, TOPCon, HJT or ABC Technology

Newer high-efficiency technologies can have different prices from older P-Type or PERC products.

A-Grade and Documentation

Ask for the exact model number, serial number, warranty documentation and grade. Investigate unusually low quotations.

Wholesale vs Retail

Large installers and distributors may receive different rates from a homeowner buying a small number of panels.

Currency and Supply

Imported solar equipment can be affected by currency movements, shipping, supply and local inventory.

How Much Do 5kW Solar Panels Cost?

A 5kW array represents about 5,000 watts of panel capacity. At Rs. 40 per watt, the panel-only estimate is 5,000W × Rs. 40 = Rs. 200,000. At Rs. 45 per watt it is Rs. 225,000. A complete system also needs an inverter, mounting structure, cables, protection equipment, labour and other components.
