What Is the EG4 12kPV?

The EG4 12kPV is a 48V all-in-one hybrid inverter designed to combine solar generation, battery storage and AC power management in one system. It can be used in residential and other solar-plus-storage installations where energy management and battery backup are important.

One of the important points to understand is the difference between PV input capacity and AC output capacity. The EG4 12kPV can utilize up to approximately 12kW of solar PV input, while its continuous AC output is rated at approximately 8kW.

EG4 12kPV Solar & Battery System

The inverter is designed for solar-plus-storage systems where solar panels charge a 48V battery bank while the inverter manages AC loads. Its hybrid architecture makes it suitable for applications that require grid interaction, battery backup or off-grid operation.

Solar Input

The EG4 12kPV provides approximately 12kW of PV input capacity with two MPPT trackers. Each MPPT supports up to 25A, allowing the system designer to divide the solar array across two independent tracking inputs.

Battery System

The inverter uses a 48V battery platform and supports communication with compatible battery systems. Battery compatibility and communication requirements should always be checked before installation.

Parallel Operation

Multiple compatible units can be configured in parallel. The system supports up to 10 units, allowing a significantly larger overall AC capacity for suitable installations.
