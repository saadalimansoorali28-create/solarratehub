Enphase IQ Battery 5P is an all-in-one AC-coupled storage system with integrated microinverters, built for reliable backup power, high performance, and modular expansion within compatible Enphase Energy System configurations.

Expansion & Future Capacity

The IQ Battery 5P is modular, allowing compatible systems to add additional battery units as storage needs grow. Final system sizing depends on the Enphase configuration, backup requirements, electrical design and local installation rules.
