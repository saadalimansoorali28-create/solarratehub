UK plug-in solar rules: what buyers need to know

  • Great Britain: the new plug-in solar route took effect on 27 August 2026.
  • Product compliance matters: the socket route is for qualifying products that meet the required safety specification.
  • Up to 800W: the government's launch information says each plug-in solar kit can produce up to 800W.
  • No battery in the basic plug-in route: the government consultation describes plug-in solar systems without batteries connected to a standard mains socket.
  • Check your exact model: don't assume that every 800W kit sold online is legal to plug into a UK household socket.

How much can plug-in solar save?

The UK government says households could save up to £110 per year, with plug-in solar potentially supplying up to 20% of average home electricity use. Your real saving depends on panel orientation, shade, household daytime consumption, electricity prices and how much generation you use directly. citeturn0search1

For a small balcony or garden system, daytime self-consumption is particularly important. A household that uses electricity while the panels are generating can normally get more direct value from the solar energy than one that is empty all day.
