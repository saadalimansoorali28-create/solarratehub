5kW Solar System Price in Pakistan 2026 – Complete Cost & Savings Guide

A 5kW solar system is a popular size for homes and small businesses in Pakistan. It can provide a meaningful amount of daytime electricity and reduce dependence on grid power. But the total project cost depends on the panels, inverter, mounting structure, wiring, protection equipment, installation and whether batteries are included.

This guide explains how to estimate a 5kW solar system price in Pakistan in 2026 without treating one dealer's quotation as a universal market price.

Important price disclaimer: Solar equipment prices change with brand, model, exchange rates, stock, dealer margins and installation requirements. The ranges below are planning estimates only. Get a current written quotation for the exact models before purchasing.

What Does a 5kW Solar System Mean?

A 5kW system generally means roughly 5,000 watts of solar panel capacity. Depending on the panel wattage, this could be around 9 panels of 585W (5,265W DC) or another combination close to 5kW. The final panel count should be selected around the inverter's permitted PV input and the roof layout.

5kW Solar Panel Calculation

If panels average Rs. 40 per watt, 5,000W of panels costs approximately:

5,000 × Rs. 40 = Rs. 200,000

At Rs. 45 per watt:

5,000 × Rs. 45 = Rs. 225,000

Using 585W modules, 9 panels would provide 5,265W of DC capacity. The actual price depends on the exact panel model.

On-Grid vs Hybrid 5kW System

On-Grid

An on-grid system is normally simpler and cheaper because it does not require a large battery bank. It is designed to work with the electricity grid and is commonly considered where grid availability and the applicable utility rules make it suitable.

Hybrid

A hybrid inverter can support battery storage and backup functionality. The battery can become the biggest additional cost, so compare battery capacity, chemistry, usable capacity, warranty and cycle specifications rather than looking only at inverter price.

How Much Electricity Can a 5kW System Generate?

Actual generation varies by city, season, panel orientation, shading, temperature, dust, system losses and equipment. As a rough planning example, a 5kW array might average around 18–24 kWh per day over a suitable annualized period in a good location, but actual daily output can be much lower on cloudy or rainy days and higher on strong-sun days.

Do not use a single generation number as a guarantee. A professional site assessment should account for local solar irradiation and roof conditions.

How Much Can a 5kW System Save?

Your savings depend mainly on how much solar electricity you actually consume, your utility tariff, net-metering/export rules, system losses and whether electricity is used during daylight. If a household uses most solar generation directly during the day, the value of that energy can be higher than if much of the generation is curtailed or exported under less favourable terms.

What Should Be Included in a 5kW Quotation?

  • Exact solar panel brand, model and wattage
  • Number of panels and total DC capacity
  • Exact inverter brand and model
  • Mounting structure material and thickness
  • DC and AC cable specifications
  • Breakers, surge protection and earthing details
  • Installation labour and transport
  • Product and installation warranties
  • Battery model and usable capacity if hybrid
  • Final price including all applicable charges

Common Mistakes When Buying a 5kW System

  1. Comparing only the total price.
  2. Ignoring the exact panel model.
  3. Buying an inverter without checking PV input limits.
  4. Not asking about protection equipment and earthing.
  5. Assuming advertised generation is guaranteed.
  6. Ignoring shading and roof orientation.
  7. Not checking warranty terms and after-sales support.

5kW Solar System vs 5kW Solar Panels

These terms are not always identical. A quotation called a “5kW solar system” should normally include the inverter and balance-of-system components. “5kW solar panels” refers to the panel capacity only. Always ask the seller to separate panel cost from the complete system cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 5kW solar system price in Pakistan in 2026?

A broad planning estimate for a complete 5kW system without a large battery bank can be roughly Rs. 450,000–740,000+, depending on equipment and installation. Exact dealer prices vary.

How many panels are needed for 5kW?

It depends on panel wattage. For example, nine 585W panels total 5,265W, while ten 550W panels total 5,500W. The inverter and roof layout also matter.

Is a 5kW hybrid system worth it?

It can be useful when backup power and battery storage are important. Compare the added battery cost with the value of backup and the amount of solar energy you want to store.

How long does a 5kW solar system last?

Solar panels are designed for long service lives, while inverters, batteries and other electronics have different warranty and replacement periods. Check each component separately.
