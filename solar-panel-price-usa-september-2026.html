USA Solar Panel Price Per Watt — September 2026

Recent September 2026 market data shows why it is important to label the price correctly. The PES Solar Price Index for the week of September 2 reported an all-panel composite median of about $0.33/W. Its residential mono 370–460W category was around $0.34/W, while TOPCon and bifacial benchmarks were around $0.41/W.

Recent U.S.-assembled module data has also placed the median around $0.30/W. At the same time, U.S. module spot-market reporting has put some prices above $0.40/W. These figures represent different market segments and should not be presented as one universal retail price.

Why Are U.S. Solar Panel Prices Moving in 2026?

U.S. solar pricing is being shaped by module supply, domestic manufacturing, import conditions and trade policy. September market data shows a noticeable difference between broad wholesale benchmarks and some U.S. spot-market prices, so buyers should not assume that one quoted price per watt represents the entire market.

Trade measures are also changing the economics of imported solar equipment. Section 232 developments have been one factor behind higher U.S. module pricing, while domestic manufacturing and changing supply conditions can influence future prices.

Buying tip: When comparing installers, ask for the exact panel model and wattage, inverter model, battery capacity if included, system size, cash price, financing price, warranties and complete scope of work. Compare the total project, not only the panel price.

FAQ — USA Solar Panel Prices September 2026

What is the solar panel price per watt in the USA in September 2026?

Current market references vary by segment. A September 2026 composite benchmark is about $0.33/W, residential mono around $0.34/W, and TOPCon/bifacial around $0.41/W. Some U.S. spot-market prices have been reported above $0.40/W. These are market benchmarks, not universal retail prices.

How much does a solar panel cost by itself?

For planning, panel-only pricing can fall roughly in the $0.27–$0.50+/W range depending on technology, brand, sourcing and market segment. A specific retail panel may cost more after distribution, freight and seller margin.

How much is a 10kW solar system in the USA?

A broad September 2026 planning range of $2.50–$3.50/W puts a 10kW installed system around $25,000–$35,000 before project-specific incentives or unusual site costs.

Which U.S. state has the cheapest solar?

There is no single permanent cheapest state because installer quotes and market conditions change. Current EnergySage examples include Texas at about $2.25/W, Mississippi at $2.29/W and Nevada at $2.42/W.

Does the installed price include a battery?

Not necessarily. Many solar quotes are solar-only. A battery should be listed separately with its usable capacity, power rating, warranty and installed price.

Should I buy panels separately to save money?

Not automatically. A low module price does not include design, electrical work, permitting, labor, inverter integration or warranty coordination. Compare the complete project cost and scope.

Final Takeaway

For September 2026, a useful USA panel-only reference is roughly $0.33–$0.41/W for several major market benchmarks, with some U.S. spot-market prices above $0.40/W and domestic-assembly benchmarks around $0.30/W. For homeowners, a practical installed planning range is around $2.50–$3.50/W, making a typical 10kW project roughly $25,000–$35,000 before project-specific factors.

The key is to keep panel-only price separate from installed system price. When you receive a quote, compare equipment, system size, cash price, financing, warranties and the complete installation scope before deciding.
