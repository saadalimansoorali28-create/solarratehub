Tesla Powerwall 3 vs. Enphase IQ Battery 5P: Which Solar Battery Is Best in 2026?

Updated September 2026 · SolarRateHub

Quick answer: Tesla Powerwall 3 is the stronger choice for homeowners who want a large 13.5 kWh battery, high 11.5 kW continuous output and integrated inverter in one unit. Enphase IQ Battery 5P is attractive for homes already built around Enphase equipment or homeowners who want a modular 5 kWh battery that can be expanded. The better value depends on your existing solar system, backup loads and local installation price.

Tesla Powerwall 3 price in 2026

Powerwall 3 pricing is highly location- and installer-dependent because the final project can include the battery, electrical work, gateway/system equipment, permits and other installation requirements. Current 2026 installer-market estimates put a Powerwall 3 installation roughly in the $12,000–$16,000 range, with some installers quoting higher or lower depending on the project.

One 2026 installer comparison lists a $15,400 installed example for Powerwall 3, while another 2026 cost guide gives a broader $12,000–$16,000 installed range. Treat these as market examples rather than a universal Tesla price.

Enphase IQ Battery 5P price in 2026

The IQ Battery 5P has a much smaller 5 kWh capacity per unit, so comparing one 5P directly with one Powerwall 3 is not a capacity-for-capacity comparison. Current 2026 installer-market examples put one installed IQ Battery 5P around $6,500–$9,000, while some installer quotes are higher depending on the system controller and electrical work.

For roughly 10 kWh of storage, a homeowner may need two 5P units. That can bring the installed project cost into the approximate $9,000–$12,000+ range in some markets. For three units, capacity reaches about 15 kWh, but installation requirements and pricing vary significantly.

Important: battery prices on this page are 2026 market estimates from installer and industry pricing sources, not guaranteed retail prices. Always get a local written quote before using a number for a purchase decision.

Capacity: Powerwall 3 wins on storage per unit

Powerwall 3 provides 13.5 kWh of energy capacity in one unit. The IQ Battery 5P starts at 5 kWh per unit. That means one Powerwall 3 stores about 2.7 times as much energy as one 5P.

For a home that wants overnight solar self-consumption or substantial outage coverage, the larger single-unit capacity can simplify system sizing. The Enphase advantage is modularity: you can build storage in 5 kWh steps rather than committing to one large battery.

Power output: a major difference

Power capacity is different from energy capacity. A battery can store plenty of energy but still struggle to start or run several large appliances simultaneously if its output is limited.

Tesla rates Powerwall 3 at up to 11.5 kW continuous power and 11.5 kW backup power, with 185 LRA motor-start capability. Enphase lists up to 3.84 kW continuous output per IQ Battery 5P in a single-phase configuration. Multiple 5P units can be combined for more output.

Solar integration and inverter design

Powerwall 3 integrates the inverter and system controller architecture into the battery system. Tesla says Powerwall 3 has six solar inputs with maximum power point trackers and 97.5% solar-to-grid efficiency.

The IQ Battery 5P is an AC-coupled battery. For backup applications, an Enphase IQ System Controller is part of the broader system architecture. This can be especially attractive when the home already uses Enphase microinverters and monitoring.

Warranty: Enphase has the longer term

Tesla lists a 10-year warranty for Powerwall. Enphase states a 15-year limited warranty for the IQ Battery 5P in the U.S. market. Warranty terms, capacity guarantees and installation requirements should be checked in the current product documentation before purchase.

Which battery is better for backup during a blackout?

Powerwall 3: Better suited to a homeowner who wants substantial whole-home backup from a single battery, particularly when high-power loads such as central air-conditioning, pumps or multiple appliances matter.

IQ Battery 5P: Better suited to a modular strategy. A single unit can support smaller essential loads, while multiple units can be added when more energy and power are required.

Powerwall 3 or Enphase 5P: which should you buy?

  • Choose Tesla Powerwall 3 if you want high power from one unit, 13.5 kWh capacity, an integrated inverter and a straightforward whole-home backup approach.
  • Choose Enphase IQ Battery 5P if your home already has Enphase microinverters, you prefer modular 5 kWh steps, or the longer warranty is important to you.
  • Choose multiple 5P units when you want to scale storage gradually and your installer can configure the system for the loads you need to back up.

How much battery storage does a typical home need?

A home that only wants refrigerators, lights, internet and selected outlets during an outage may need far less storage than a home trying to run central air-conditioning, an electric range, water heating and an EV charger.

Before buying, make a list of your critical loads, their running watts, startup requirements and expected outage duration. Then ask an installer to size both kWh capacity and kW power. Buying the biggest battery is not always the cheapest way to achieve the backup you actually need.

2026 bottom line

There is no universal winner. Powerwall 3 is the stronger single-battery solution for high-output whole-home backup, while Enphase IQ Battery 5P is the more modular choice and can make particular sense in an Enphase-based solar home. Price the complete installed system—not just the battery—because electrical work, backup controls and installation can change the economics substantially.

Frequently asked questions

Is Powerwall 3 better than Enphase IQ Battery 5P?

Powerwall 3 has much more energy and power per unit. The IQ 5P offers modular 5 kWh expansion and a longer U.S. warranty. The better choice depends on the home's solar equipment and backup goals.

How much does a Powerwall 3 cost in 2026?

Current installer-market examples commonly place a complete Powerwall 3 installation around $12,000–$16,000, but local quotes can differ significantly.

How much does an Enphase IQ Battery 5P cost in 2026?

Current installer-market examples commonly place one installed 5P around $6,500–$9,000, with the final cost depending on the number of batteries and required Enphase backup equipment.

Can two Enphase IQ Battery 5P units replace a Powerwall 3?

Two 5P units provide about 10 kWh of storage, which is less than Powerwall 3's 13.5 kWh. They can provide a useful backup system, but the overall power and system architecture should be sized by an installer.

Which battery has the longer warranty?

Enphase IQ Battery 5P has the longer published warranty term in the U.S.: 15 years versus 10 years for Tesla Powerwall 3.
