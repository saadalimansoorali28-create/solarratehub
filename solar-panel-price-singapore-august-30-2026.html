What Affects the Daily Solar Rate in Singapore?

  • Panel wattage: Higher-output modules generally cost more per panel.
  • Efficiency: High-efficiency technology can carry a premium.
  • Technology: TOPCon, back-contact and HJT modules can occupy different price tiers.
  • Supplier and order size: Wholesale and project quotations can differ from individual-panel prices.
  • Installation: A complete solar quotation includes more than the panels.
  • Market conditions: Currency movements, supply, logistics and manufacturer pricing can affect rates.

Solar Panel Price Per Watt in Singapore

Comparing only the price of one panel can be misleading because panel wattages are different. A better approach is to calculate the solar panel price per watt and then compare efficiency, warranty, degradation, installation quality and the total system cost.

Panel Price vs Complete Solar System Price

The daily solar rate shown above refers to the panel itself. A complete solar installation may also include an inverter, mounting structure, electrical protection, cables, monitoring equipment, engineering and professional installation. Therefore, the final system quotation can be substantially higher than the combined panel prices.

Which Solar Panel Is Best in Singapore?

There is no single best solar panel for every Singapore property. AIKO may appeal to buyers prioritizing very high efficiency, Jinko and Trina offer mainstream options, LONGi provides a broad range of high-efficiency modules, and REC targets the premium segment.

The best choice depends on your available roof area, electricity consumption, budget, installer, warranty and expected long-term energy production.

Final Takeaway: Singapore Solar Rate Today

The solar panel price in Singapore today can range from a few hundred Singapore dollars per panel to substantially more for premium modules. For August 30, 2026, the reference daily solar rate comparison covers AIKO, Jinko Solar, Trina Solar, LONGi and REC.

Before buying, request a current quotation for the exact model and compare the total installed cost rather than choosing a panel only by its headline price.
